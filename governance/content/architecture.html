Homelab architecture

Nothing listens inbound — now behind one login

Nine codebases, ten running instances, a governance site and a jobs container beside them, two Obsidian vaults, and still not a single open port at home. Since September, most of the fleet moved off direct LAN addresses onto a private bridge that only Traefik can reach, gated by a new single-login service — a stricter version of the same rule, not a different one.

Three ways nothing here listens inbound

LAN-ONLY
No public exposure at all, login required even at home. Most of the fleet — this used to mean a bearer token per app; since homelab-auth it's one shared login via Traefik's forwardAuth instead.
OUTBOUND RELAY
A tiny Fly.io relay is the only internet-facing piece — the NAS-side worker polls it outbound for jobs and posts results the same way. Taster only; its one bearer key still drives routine phone use with no admin split to gate.
PRIVATE-IP HTTPS
A real Let's Encrypt cert via DNS-01 for a public name resolving to a private address — lets a cloud-hosted PWA iframe the home app, without a port. Family Calendar only, for its fixed-IP e-ink hardware.
